================================================================================
2. COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE & WORKFLOW
================================================================================

1. VIDEO INGESTION & PIPELINE:
   - Video Files: Uploaded via frontend -> saved in 'storage/uploads/' -> queued
     for asynchronous processing by 'services/video_processor.py'.
   - Live Streams: Captured via OpenCV in 'services/camera_streamer.py' and streamed
     as multipart MJPEG over HTTP.

2. VISION & AI INFERENCE:
   - Detection: 'ai/yolo_detector.py' detects vehicles (car, motorcycle, bus, truck).
   - Tracking: 'vision/tracker.py' associates detections across consecutive frames
     to maintain consistent IDs and motion vectors.
   - Counting: 'vision/counting_line.py' checks line intersection to register directional
     inflow/outflow counts.
   - Incidents: 'vision/incident_detector.py' detects vehicles driving against permitted
     vectors (wrong-way) or remaining stationary for threshold durations (stopped vehicle).
   - Density: 'vision/density_estimator.py' measures occupancy ratio and queue length.

3. PERSISTENCE & DATA LAYER:
   - Database: SQLite database ('traffic_system.db') managed by SQLAlchemy ORM.
   - Entities: Cameras, Videos, VideoAnalysis, TrafficMetrics, VehicleCounts, Incidents,
     Alerts, SignalPlans, and SystemLogs.

4. OPTIMIZATION & FORECASTING:
   - Signals: 'services/signal_optimizer.py' computes Webster's optimum cycle length:
     C_opt = (1.5*L + 5) / (1 - Y) and allocates green times per approach (North, South,
     East, West) proportionally to critical lane flow ratios.
   - Prediction: 'ai/predictor.py' applies Holt-Winters exponential smoothing to forecast
     traffic volumes and queue lengths for future time intervals.

5. PRESENTATION LAYER:
   - FastAPI server hosts REST endpoints and WebSocket channels ('/ws/monitoring').
   - Fully vanilla JavaScript frontend consumes REST APIs and real-time WebSockets
     to update Chart.js graphs and live telemetry HUD displays.
